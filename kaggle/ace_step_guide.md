# ACE-Step 1.5 — music guide

Goal: one instrumental score ~80s for ducking under narration.

## Inputs (from repo)

See `outputs/music/ace_step_brief.json` after `npm run music`:

- caption (style / mood / texture)
- durationSec, tempoBpm
- instrumental: true
- cues

## Practice

1. Generate **3** candidates (different seeds).
2. Audition under the radio cut at low volume.
3. Prefer gentle texture, no strong lead melody under voice.
4. Save the winner as `outputs/music/score.wav`.
5. Log choice in `EXPERIMENTS.md` (E06).
